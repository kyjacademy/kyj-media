Privacy Policy

KYJ Academy Uploader · Effective date: 2026-11-19

1. What this app is

KYJ Academy Uploader is an internal tool operated by 김유진아카데미 (KYJ Academy). It publishes videos produced by KYJ Academy to KYJ Academy's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Information we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the connected account (open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload and publish KYJ Academy's own videos.
  • Publishing results returned by TikTok (post ID, status).

We do not access, collect or store information about any other TikTok user, including viewers, followers or commenters.

3. Storage and sharing

Tokens and publishing records are stored only on KYJ Academy's own computer. They are not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and are not used for advertising or profiling.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the connection is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); the stored tokens are then deleted.

5. Contact

Instagram / Threads: @kyjacademy
